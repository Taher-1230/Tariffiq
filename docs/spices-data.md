# Spices Tariff Data & Architecture Documentation — Chapter 09 (Headings 0904–0910)

## Overview

This document describes the machine-readable dataset and structural specification for Spices under Chapter 09 of the Harmonized System / Indian ITC(HS) schedule, covering Headings **0904 through 0910**.

All data was extracted directly and without fabrication from the authoritative working reference:
- **Source Document**: `HS PROJECT OVERVIEW.pdf` (Chapter 09 Master Reference, pages 4–12)
- **Source Section**: Headings 0904, 0905, 0906, 0907, 0908, 0909, 0910

## 3. Major Classification Attributes

The authoritative source differentiates tariff lines across Headings 0904–0910 based strictly on the following factual attributes:

1. **Botanical / Commodity Family**:
   - Pepper (*Piper*), *Capsicum*, *Pimenta*, Vanilla, Cinnamon (*Cinnamomum zeylanicum* Blume, Cassia), Cloves, Nutmeg, Mace, Cardamom (*Amomum*, *Elettaria*), Coriander, Cumin, Anise, Badian, Caraway, Fennel, Juniper Berries, Ginger, Saffron, Turmeric (*Curcuma*), Celery, Fenugreek, Dill, Ajwain, *Cassia torea*, Poppy, Mustard.
2. **Crushed / Ground Status**:
   - Distinct subheadings at 6-digit level distinguish "neither crushed nor ground" from "crushed or ground" / "powder" across almost all headings (0904, 0905, 0906, 0907, 0908, 0909, 0910).
3. **Form / Physical Presentation**:
   - *Bark* vs *tree flowers* (Cinnamon 0906)
   - *Whole fruit* vs *stem* (Cloves 0907)
   - *In shell* vs *shelled* (Nutmeg 0908)
   - *Powder*, *seeds*, *husk* (Cardamom 0908, Other Spices 0910)
   - *Stigma* vs *stamen* (Saffron 0910)
   - *Seed* vs *powder* vs *husk* (0910 99)
4. **Processing State / Moisture**:
   - *Fresh* vs *dried* (Ginger 0910, Turmeric 0910)
   - *Bleached* vs *unbleached* (Ginger 0910, Cardamom 0908)
   - *Dehydrated* vs *frozen* (Green pepper 0904)
   - *Garbled* vs *ungarbled* (Black pepper 0904)
   - *Extracted* vs *not extracted* (Cloves 0907)
5. **Variety / Regional Distinction**:
   - *Alleppey green* vs *Coorg green* (Small cardamom 0908)
   - *Long pepper*, *light black pepper*, *pinheads* (Pepper 0904)
   - *Black cumin* vs *cumin other than black* (Cumin 0909)
6. **Quality Grade**:
   - *Of seed quality* vs *other* (Coriander, Cumin, Anise, Badian, Caraway/Fennel, Juniper berries under 0909)
7. **Single Ingredient vs Mixture**:
   - Same-heading mixtures remain in heading (Chapter Note 1a)
   - Cross-heading spice mixtures classify under `09109100` (Chapter Note 1b)

## 4. Legal Notes & Mixture Rules

The PDF explicitly establishes four statutory chapter notes and two supplementary notes that govern Chapter 09 classification:

1. **Chapter Note 1(a)**: Mixtures of two or more products of the same heading remain in that heading.
2. **Chapter Note 1(b)**: Mixtures of two or more products of different headings within 0904 to 0910 go to heading 0910 (`09109100`).
3. **Essential Character Rule (Note 2)**: The addition of other substances does not automatically remove classification from Chapter 09 if the resulting goods retain the essential character of the spices.
4. **Condiment / Seasoning Exclusion (Note 3)**: If the resulting product no longer retains the essential character of Chapter 09 goods, it is excluded from Chapter 09; mixed condiments or mixed seasonings go to heading **2103**.
5. **Cubeb Pepper Exclusion (Note 4)**: Chapter 09 does not cover cubeb pepper (*Piper cubeba*) or other products of heading **1211**.
6. **Supplementary Note 1**: "Spice" includes vegetable products rich in essential oils and aromatic principles used mainly as condiments (whole, crushed, or powdered).
7. **Supplementary Note 2**: "Masalas" may remain under spices provided they retain the essential character of spices.

## 6. Unsupported Assumptions (DO NOT INVENT)

1. **Weight / Package Size Thresholds**: Unlike Tea (0902), Spices tariff lines contain **zero** net weight thresholds (e.g. 25g, 1kg, 3kg, 20kg). No weight logic should be imported.
2. **Bulk vs Retail Presentation**: Unlike Coffee (0901), Spices tariff lines do not have broad bulk vs retail packing distinctions (except specific items like bulk vs packet which are not present in 0904–0910).
3. **Seed Quality Definition**: The source specifies "of seed quality" vs "other" for seeds under 0909 without defining the exact germination/purity standards. The classifier must prompt the user rather than guessing.
4. **Essential Character Thresholds**: The source notes legal essential character rules for mixtures and seasonings, but does not provide mathematical percentage thresholds. This requires legal/human signoff.
